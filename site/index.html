Frequently Asked Questions

Is Breadify really 100% free?

+

Yes! Breadify is completely free to download and use. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no premium features locked behind a paywall. We believe bread joy should be available to everyone!

How do I customize my bread notification time?

+

Simply go to Settings > Notification Preferences and set your preferred time. You can have different times for weekdays and weekends too!

When will Breadify be available on iOS?

+

We're currently working on our iOS version and expect to launch within the next few months. Sign up for our newsletter to be notified as soon as it's available!

Do you have bread recipes as well?

+

While we don't currently include detailed recipes, we're working on adding this feature in a future update. For now, we focus on bread varieties, history, and fun facts.
